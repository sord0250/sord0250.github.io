AI

My AI Approach

Understand first

Before building, I make sure I understand the context, the system, and the security picture. I want to know why something works, not just that it does.

Then dive in

Once I understand the problem, I get started and iterate quickly, letting AI agents handle the heavy lifting of research, drafting, and code.

Decisions stay mine

AI recommends options and plans from my direction, but I make the calls. Anything crucial, I do myself to make sure it's right.

Security-check the output

I run AI-driven security reviews against the code AI generates, using skills such as an OWASP Top 10 review, so speed never comes at the cost of security.
